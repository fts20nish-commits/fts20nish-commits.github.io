Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Intent is a personal desktop app used only by its owner. This page explains what it does with data when you sign in with Google.

What Intent accesses

  • Your Google account email, to show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive, with the drive.file permission only. Intent can see and change only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Where data goes

  • Files you choose to back up are stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder named "Intent".
  • App data (notes, wishlist, trades, settings) is backed up to a private database owned by you. Only your signed-in account can read it.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your own computer.

What Intent never does

  • It does not sell, share or send your data to anyone else.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • Google user data is used only to provide backup in the app, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use Disconnect in Intent's Cloud window, or remove Intent at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files Intent made stay in your Drive until you delete them.
